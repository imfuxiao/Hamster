# Licensing History / 许可证变更历史

## 1. License History / 许可证历史

Hamster was originally released under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0), and the [v2.1.0 source tag](https://github.com/imfuxiao/Hamster/blob/2.1.0/LICENSE.txt) contains that license. On February 26, 2025, [commit `8ab16ce`](https://github.com/imfuxiao/Hamster/commit/8ab16ce7688029df8036097203e4470ac2ea3894) changed the license notice on the main branch to MIT while retaining historical contributions. For example, the [Shengbi keyboard layout contribution](https://github.com/imfuxiao/Hamster/commit/9780d0658aed853a3cd68e9ae3da026065ae257c) is present in both v2.1.0 and the commit that introduced the MIT notice.

Hamster 最初按 GPL-3.0 发布，[v2.1.0 源码标签](https://github.com/imfuxiao/Hamster/blob/2.1.0/LICENSE.txt)也包含该许可证。2025 年 2 月 26 日，[提交 `8ab16ce`](https://github.com/imfuxiao/Hamster/commit/8ab16ce7688029df8036097203e4470ac2ea3894)将主分支的许可证声明改为 MIT，当时仍保留了历史贡献。例如，[声笔键盘布局的贡献](https://github.com/imfuxiao/Hamster/commit/9780d0658aed853a3cd68e9ae3da026065ae257c)同时存在于 v2.1.0 和引入 MIT 声明的提交中。

This review covers the public Git history. Whether later App Store builds contain those contributions still needs to be verified against their corresponding source.

以上核查依据公开 Git 历史。后续 App Store 版本是否仍包含这些贡献，需核对各版本的对应源码。

## 2. Contributor Relicensing / 贡献者重新授权

The maintainer confirmed in [issue #801](https://github.com/imfuxiao/Hamster/issues/801#issuecomment-7151078839) that historical contributors were not asked for consent before the MIT notice was introduced. The project is now requesting permission from the relevant rights holders to offer their contributions under MIT in addition to GPL-3.0.

项目维护者已在 [#801](https://github.com/imfuxiao/Hamster/issues/801#issuecomment-7151078839) 确认，引入 MIT 声明前未征询历史贡献者的同意。项目现向相关权利人征询，是否同意在 GPL-3.0 之外同时以 MIT 授权其贡献。

- Relicensing discussion / 授权征询 [GitHub issue #802](https://github.com/imfuxiao/Hamster/issues/802)
- Response deadline / 征询截止时间 2027-01-31 23:59 (UTC+8)

Participation is voluntary. Silence does not constitute consent. Each grant applies only to the contributions and effective period covered by the rights holder's permission.

补充授权征询遵循自愿原则，未回复不视为同意授权。每份授权适用的贡献范围和生效时间以权利人的许可为准。

## 3. Contributions Without Relicensing Consent / 未取得重新授权的贡献

The maintainer will review the relevant pull requests and subsequent changes for contributions whose rights holders decline or whose explicit permission is still outstanding at the deadline.

Before any future version can be distributed under MIT alone, contributions that require additional permission must be covered by that permission, removed, or independently reimplemented, and the applicable terms for third-party material must be satisfied.

对于明确不同意重新授权，或截至征询截止日期仍未取得明确授权的贡献，项目维护者将审查相关贡献及其后续代码变更。

如未来版本拟仅按 MIT 分发，须先对需要额外授权的贡献取得许可、移除相关内容或独立重新实现，并满足第三方内容的适用许可条款。

The deadline is an administrative deadline for the consent process and does not alter any existing copyright or licensing obligations.

上述截止日期仅为授权征询的管理期限，不改变任何既有的著作权或许可证义务。

## 4. Copyright and Attribution / 著作权及贡献者署名

Copyright in individual contributions remains with their respective copyright holders unless otherwise agreed.

Relicensing does not constitute a transfer of copyright ownership.

除非另有约定，各项贡献的著作权仍归其相应著作权人所有。重新授权不涉及著作权转让。

Applicable copyright notices and license notices shall be preserved in accordance with the relevant license requirements.

项目将依据适用的许可证要求，保留相关版权声明及许可声明。

Historical contributors are acknowledged in [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md), regardless of whether they have consented to relicensing or whether their contributions remain in subsequent versions.

历史贡献者均在 [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md) 中予以署名，不因其是否同意重新授权，或其贡献是否保留在后续版本中而受到影响。

Inclusion in the contributor list does not imply consent to relicensing.

列入贡献者名单不代表其已同意许可证变更。

## 5. Third-Party Components / 第三方组件

Third-party components and incorporated third-party code remain subject to their respective applicable licenses and copyright notices.

The project's license transition does not independently alter the licensing terms of third-party components.

项目所使用的第三方组件及引入的第三方代码，仍受其各自适用的许可证及版权声明约束。

项目自身的许可证变更不会单方面改变第三方组件的授权条款。

## 6. Scope of Existing Grants / 既有授权的适用范围

The MIT grant in [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt) covers the portions that xiao.fu has the right to license under MIT. Separate MIT grants from other rights holders apply within their stated scope. Previously granted GPL-3.0 rights and applicable obligations continue to apply, and the license text is available in [LICENSES/GPL-3.0.txt](LICENSES/GPL-3.0.txt).

[LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt) 中的 MIT 授权适用于 xiao.fu 有权按 MIT 授权的内容。其他权利人另行授予的 MIT 许可按各自的授权范围适用。既有 GPL-3.0 权利和适用义务继续有效，正文见 [LICENSES/GPL-3.0.txt](LICENSES/GPL-3.0.txt)。

## 7. Contact / 联系方式

For questions regarding copyright, licensing, attribution, or historical contributions, please contact the project maintainer through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/imfuxiao/Hamster/issues).

如对著作权、许可证、贡献者署名或历史贡献存在任何疑问，欢迎通过 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/imfuxiao/Hamster/issues) 联系项目维护者。

Thank you to everyone who has contributed to Hamster.

感谢所有曾经为 Hamster（仓输入法）作出贡献的开发者。
